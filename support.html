RevoSing · Privacy · Terms · Support

Support

Contact RevoSing

Email hoangnamttq@gmail.com for help with Reverse Audio: RevoSing. Include app version/build (Settings → About), iOS version and what happened. Screenshots are helpful if they contain no private information. We do not need passwords, verification codes, payment card information or private recordings. This page and the bundled help remain available without a RevoSing account.

Microphone access

Start a recording to see the permission request. If access was denied, open iOS Settings → RevoSing → Microphone. Return to the app and try again. You can still import an audio file if you prefer not to allow microphone access.

Import, export and sharing

Import a supported audio file using the Files picker. Files protected by digital rights management cannot be decoded. Keep the original outside RevoSing as a backup. If export fails, check free device storage, shorten the selection or try M4A audio. If sharing is cancelled, your recording remains in Library; export again when ready.

Purchases and subscriptions

Use Settings → Restore purchases with the Apple Account that made the purchase. If a payment is pending, wait for Apple to confirm it. Manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancellation preserves access through the paid period. Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com. Never buy again merely because a restore is taking time.

Ads and privacy

Paid Pro or Remove Ads access removes advertising. If ads remain after a verified purchase, restore purchases and contact support with app version/build. Use Settings → Ad privacy choices for available consent options. An unavailable ad does not block free audio features.

Data and recovery

Your audio is stored locally. Clearing render cache does not delete original recordings or exports. Export important files before deleting the app. If recording is interrupted, reopen Library and look for the recovered recording. Support cannot remotely recover deleted device files.
