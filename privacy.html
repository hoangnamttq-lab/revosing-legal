Privacy Policy

Your audio stays on your device

RevoSing records, reverses and edits audio on your iPhone. We do not run an audio upload, transcription or cloud library service. Recordings, imported originals, edits and exports are stored locally. Sharing sends only the file you select to the destination you choose. Your device backup settings may include these files.

Permissions and control

Microphone permission is requested when you start recording. You can change permission in iOS Settings. Import uses the iOS file picker; RevoSing copies only the selected file. Export uses the system share sheet. RevoSing does not request your contacts or precise location.

Advertising and privacy choices

The free version uses Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform to display ads and manage consent. The ad SDK may process IP-derived approximate location, device or app identifiers, ad interactions, usage and diagnostic information for advertising, fraud prevention and service measurement. It does not receive your recordings or their names from RevoSing. We request non-personalized ads and do not request access to the advertising identifier through App Tracking Transparency. Non-personalized ads can still process data. Where required, a consent form appears before ads can be requested; use Settings → Ad privacy choices to review available choices. RevoSing remains usable if ads or consent services are unavailable. Owner QA uses Google test ad units. An active Pro subscription or Remove Ads purchase stops further ad requests and removes ads.

Purchases and diagnostics

Apple processes in-app purchases. RevoSing checks Apple-verified transactions to deliver access; we do not receive card details. Basic feature counters used inside the app are held locally and are not sent to an analytics server. Google and Apple may separately process service diagnostics under their policies.

Retention, deletion and support

You can delete individual recordings from Library. Clearing render cache preserves original recordings and exports. Removing the app can remove its local files; export anything you want to keep first. We cannot recover local audio that you delete. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send and retain it only as needed to respond and resolve the request. Do not send private audio, passwords or payment details. Contact support for questions or requests concerning correspondence.

Children and changes

RevoSing is a general-audience audio tool, not a service directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through support. Contact us if you believe such information was sent. This policy may change as features change; the effective date appears below.
